General Test

A general test gives you a snapshot of how your app responds to a realistic task at a specific point in its development. Use it as a checkpoint, even if your app is still a rough draft.

Baseline Test

  1. Choose a realistic task your AI app is designed to support. Create a fictional student response, classroom scenario, or teacher request. Do not use real student information.
  2. Open your app and complete the task using your original scenario. Save your app’s response as your baseline.

Workbook

Open the Practice Page in your workbook and answer the questions in Sections 1 and 2.

Section 1: App Interaction & Output

  1. What was your initial input to the App?
  2. What did the App give you?
  3. How did you know when the interaction was “complete”?
  4. Is this result what you were hoping for? Why or why not?
  5. How challenging was it to use the App?
  6. What made it challenging to use?

Section 2: Reflection

Reflect on the output and consider its classroom implications.

  1. How does the output inform your instruction?
  2. What worked well? Where are the opportunities?
  3. Where might there be risks? What is the App assuming?
  4. What did it leave out that would have made this more pedagogically aligned?
  5. Where did the output surprise you?
  6. Where did the result disappoint you?
  7. How did this App impact your math instruction?
  8. What will you do with this output?
