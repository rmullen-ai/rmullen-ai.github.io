November Cycle

Coaching Session

Reconnect as a learning community, make a quick revision to your app, and use a general test to see how it’s working right now.

Community agreements

  • Stay engaged and curious.
  • Stories stay, learning leaves.
  • Be mindful of airtime and promote equity of voice.
  • Recognize power dynamics. Be aware of your own power and privilege and how to use it.
  • Acknowledge intent, prioritize impact, and keep the conversation going.
  • Treat disagreement as a positive opportunity for learning.
  • Be respectful and mindful of our own and others’ strengths and growing edges.

Session agenda and resources

  1. Welcome & Introduction

    Check in with one another and share how you’re feeling today.

    Which Blob are you feeling like? ↗
  2. Build & Revise (15 minutes)

    Revisit your app and remind yourself what you designed it to do. Make any small edits you planned before this session, then publish a rough draft so it’s ready for the general test. A work in progress is expected.

  3. Whole-Group Share-Out

    Share with the whole group. What went well during the revision sprint? What are you still working on?

  4. Model a General Test

    We’ll walk through how to run a general test and reflect on the app’s response. A general test gives us a checkpoint in the design process by showing how the app responds right now. Try it even if your app is still a rough draft.

  5. Conduct a General Test

    Run a general test of your app, then open the Practice Page in your workbook and respond to the questions in Sections 1 and 2.

  6. Group Reflection

    Use your responses in Sections 1 and 2 to guide what you share with the group.

    • What did you ask your app to do, and how did it respond?
    • What worked as intended? What surprised you or raised a question?
    • What’s one change you’d like to make or one question you’d like to explore next?
