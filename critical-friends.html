Critical Friends

Swap apps with a colleague and try theirs the way a teacher would. Your feedback helps your Critical Friend see what their app does well and what to revise next.

Steps

  1. Pair up with your Critical Friend and swap apps. Select your name and theirs in the form below.
  2. Ask your Critical Friend what problem their app is designed to solve and what kind of task to try.
  3. Run their app on a realistic task using a fictional student response, classroom scenario, or teacher request. Do not use real student information.
  4. Explore the outputs. Notice where the app upholds high-quality math teaching and learning, including the NCTM practices, and where it could improve.
  5. Share what stood out and any ideas for improvement in the form below.

Section 4: Critical Friendship Feedback

As a Critical Friend, explore the app, then use the space below to share what stood out and any ideas for improvement. Your answers save on this device as you type.
